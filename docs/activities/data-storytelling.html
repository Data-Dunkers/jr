Story recipe

  1. Claim: What do you want us to notice?
  2. Evidence: Which number from the chart supports it?
  3. So what? Why should your audience care?
  4. Be fair: What is one limitation?

Sentence starters

“The data shows that…”

“For example, the average at ___ ft was ___.”

“This matters because…”

“One limitation is…”

Headline test

Would your headline still be fair if someone saw the whole chart? If not, make it smaller and more precise.
