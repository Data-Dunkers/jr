What to do

  1. Choose a measurement.
  2. Write down the mean and median.
  3. Look for an outlier — a value far from the rest.
  4. Decide which summary is most useful and explain why.

Three summaries

Mean: add all values, divide by how many.

Median: the middle value when sorted.

Mode: the value that appears most often.

Hint

If the mean and median are very different, an outlier may be pulling the mean.
