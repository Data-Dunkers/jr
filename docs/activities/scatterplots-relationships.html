What to do

  1. Choose two measurements that might be related.
  2. Describe the pattern: up, down, or no clear direction.
  3. Find a dot that does not fit the pattern.
  4. Write one possible explanation — and one reason it might be wrong.

Reading r

r near 1: strong positive pattern.

r near −1: strong negative pattern.

r near 0: weak or no straight-line pattern.

Careful!

Two measurements can move together without one causing the other. Look for a third factor that could explain both.
