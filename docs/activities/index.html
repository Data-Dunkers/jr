How activities work

Think, Write, Compare

1. Read the chart

Every activity loads live data from your event key. If the chart is empty, collect some data first.

2. Answer the questions

Your answers save automatically in this browser. Use “Copy my answers” to hand them in.

3. Explain your evidence

A strong answer names the number, the group, and what it means.
