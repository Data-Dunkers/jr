Three tricks

Truncated axis: starting above zero makes small differences look huge.

Cherry-picking: showing only the categories that support a claim.

Missing context: leaving out sample size, labels, or a fair comparison.

Fair chart checklist

Does the axis start at zero when it should? Are all groups shown? Is the sample size clear? Does the title overclaim?
