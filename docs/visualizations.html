Many relationships at once

Correlation matrix

Orange means a positive relationship, blue means a negative relationship, and pale colours mean little or no straight-line relationship. The value r runs from −1 to 1.

A correlation is a clue, not proof. Two things can move together without one causing the other.

Chart detective

Three questions to ask every chart

1. What is being compared?

Read the title and both axes. What does each bar, point, or slice stand for?

2. What is the pattern?

Is it going up, going down, clustered, or spread out? Is there an outlier?

3. What is missing?

How many players are in each group? Could a different chart change the story?
